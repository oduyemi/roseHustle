This is a custom website for Hustlengrind Hackerhouse.

Hustlengrind Hackerhouse is a cohort based building system for CEOs in African based post-revenue startups. It focuses on key areas for growth such as Product Market Fit, Sales, Marketing, and Fundraising. Convened by Adewale Yusuf, Co-organized by Rachael Onoja, Roseline Morenikeji, and Yemi Oduyemi.

Adewale Yusuf is the Co-founder and CEO ofAltSchool Africa, TalentQL, and Chairman of Businesspoint (founded as Techpoint Africa). His work centers on building Africa’s digital workforce through education, talent development, and ecosystem building.
